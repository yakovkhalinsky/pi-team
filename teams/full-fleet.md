# Full Fleet (default)

The paper's six-role fleet as §1 describes it: "the Dispatcher decides who acts; the Builder produces artefacts; the Runtime operates live systems; the Verifier gates acceptance; the Researcher reduces uncertainty before decisions; and the Archivist maintains durable memory."

## Roster

| Role | Profile name | Thinking | Owns stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team Lead | team-lead | high | Goal receipt; Hand-off or closure |
| Dispatcher | dispatcher | high | Routing and assignment |
| Researcher | researcher | high | Context gathering (when uncertainty is high) |
| Builder | builder | medium | Action (artefact production) |
| Runtime | runtime | high | Action (live-system execution) |
| Verifier | verifier | high | Verification |
| Archivist | archivist | medium | Recording and archival |

## Use when

Any goal that touches more than one paper role. The paper names this team explicitly as "a six-role operations fleet"; for anything beyond a single-stage task, this is the default.

## Skipping stages is an anti-pattern

The paper: "Skipping a stage is an anti-pattern. The most expensive mistakes we have made came from treating context gathering or verification as optional." The only exception is Stage 3 — the paper says "the Researcher leads when uncertainty is high; otherwise the owner consults the Archivist," which means a goal with low uncertainty can move from Stage 2 directly to Stage 4.
